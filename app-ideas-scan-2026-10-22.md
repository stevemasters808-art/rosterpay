# App market scan, 22 October 2026: more small-app ideas

Four parallel scans of Google Play, the App Store and AppGoblin, each looking for one of four signals: (a) an abandoned or poorly rated incumbent, (b) paywall backlash in the newest reviews, (c) an ad-flooded leader with no banner-only rival, (d) a platform gap. Domains: trades and DIY calculators, sport and hobby scorers and timers, simple personal loggers, Australia-specific utilities. The 29 ideas from the Claude and Codex reports were excluded. Raw scan tables are in the four `scan-*.md` files alongside this one.

## Method limits

- No Reddit or Whirlpool evidence in any scan. reddit.com is blocked from this environment and no search returned threads. Every user quote is a store review.
- Play listing pages truncate through the normal fetcher; two agents pulled listings and newest-first reviews with curl, one used AppGoblin estimates only. Installs are Play bands.
- The Australian Play storefront shows different ratings from the US one for the same listing (for example ElectroMission 1.0 AU vs 3.7 US). US ratings are used unless marked AU.
- AppGoblin monthly-active figures are estimates, and they run at 1 to 8 percent of installs for every utility checked. That ratio caps ad revenue everywhere in this list.

## Scores

Same scales as the two reports. Ease 5 is pure arithmetic or UI with no permissions and static data. Commercial is for ads plus a one-off purchase, Android first, for an Australian solo developer. Revenue per 1,000 monthly actives uses the Claude report's base yields: about AUD 20 to 40 for a tier-1 audience with multi-minute sessions, under AUD 10 for a once-a-week glance.

## What this changes in the plan

The earlier second-app pick, the AS/NZS 3008 cable calculator, becomes one calculator inside idea 1 rather than its own app. Four separate paywall, subscription, stale and ad-flood signals in the same Australian trade-calculator space are a stronger case than one abandoned electrical app, and bundling keeps the portfolio clear of Play's Repetitive Content rule.

Idea 2 is the only thing found in any of the research with workout-length foreground sessions and a live refugee moment. It does not fit the "Australian audience" pattern, but a 25-minute session with a persistent banner earns more per user than any Australian once-a-week tool. It is also the fastest build on the list. If only one more app gets built this year, this is the pure-ads candidate; idea 1 is the ads-plus-purchase candidate.

## Geography note (added after owner said the market need not be Australian)

The scores above weighted Australia because both source reports assumed an Australian developer. With the US allowed, idea 2 (interval timer) moves to first: its audience is US-heavy fitness users, and US Android eCPMs (Appodeal Q4 2024: banner US$0.60, interstitial US$12.3) are the highest in the table. Idea 1 stays second and should gain an imperial feet-inch mode later so it also covers the US tradie market (Snappy 500K+ and Winograd 1M+ both carry fresh ad-flood complaints). Asia helps only for Japan, Korea, Taiwan and Singapore, and only with a native-language listing. India and South-East Asia produce installs at a yield near zero (APAC blend banner US$0.15, India well below that), which is why the cricket scorer stays dropped.

## Cheapest tests

**Builder's calc pack.** Web first, same as the penalty checker: a static page per calculator (stair stringer, rafter length, concrete volume, decking boards) with metric inputs, AU spelling, and SEO titles such as "stair stringer calculator Australia" and "rafter length calculator metric degrees". Analytics plus an Android waitlist button. Pass at week 12: 800 organic sessions a month, 35 percent completing a calculation, 10 percent 30-day return, 100 waitlist sign-ups. Then a Play open test. Kill: Chippy restores its free calculators, or Ubersuggest shows the metric calculator cluster under 500 Australian searches a month.

**Interval timer.** No web page; this is a Play product. Ship a minimal Android build in two to three weeks: rounds, work and rest, warm-up and cool-down, saved presets, voice and vibration cues, screen stays on during a workout, one banner anchored at the bottom, no interstitials ever, AUD 3 one-off remove-ads. Listing copy targets "interval timer free no subscription" and "boxing round timer". Pass over 60 days: 1,000 organic installs, 30-day retention 15 percent, median session over 15 minutes, rating 4.5 from 30. Fail: under 300 installs or sessions under 5 minutes. Kill: Timer Plus reverts to free.

**Poker blind timer.** Play-only, landscape tablet layout, levels, breaks, chip-up prompts, banner only on the between-levels screen. Pass over 60 days: 300 organic installs, median session over 90 minutes, 10 unprompted ratings at 4.3 or higher. Fail: under 100 installs.

## What would reorder this list

A Reddit or Whirlpool pass on ideas 1 and 2 (the scans had none). Ubersuggest volumes for the metric trade-calculator terms in Australia. A check of whether Timer Plus's backlash is still visible in a month. Written confirmation from one state that a third-party learner log is accepted at the practical test, which would move idea 5 up two points.
